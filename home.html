How to Play

Learn in 3 simple steps

01

Draw & Deal

Draw 2 cards each turn. Play up to 3 cards from your hand — properties, money, or action cards.

02

Build Sets

Collect complete color property sets. Use wildcards to complete tricky sets faster than your opponents.

03

Charge Rent & Win

Play action cards to charge rent, steal properties, or block attacks. First to 3 complete sets wins!
